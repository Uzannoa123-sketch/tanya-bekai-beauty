Treatments

Open a treatment to see what it helps with and how long it takes. Prices are confirmed at your consultation, once we know what your skin needs.

  • Signature facial Deep cleanse, gentle exfoliation and hydration 60 min

    A calm, thorough facial adapted to your skin on the day. A good first treatment if you're new to the clinic.

    • Clears congested pores
    • Restores moisture and glow
    • No downtime
  • Chemical peel Resurfaces dull, uneven or marked skin 45 min

    A controlled exfoliation that removes damaged surface cells so fresher skin can come through. Strength is chosen for your skin type.

    • Evens pigmentation and tone
    • Softens fine lines and acne marks
    • Light flaking for a few days
  • Microneedling Encourages collagen to firm and smooth skin 60 min

    Fine needles create tiny channels in the skin, prompting it to repair and rebuild collagen. Usually done as a course of three.

    • Improves texture and scarring
    • Reduces the look of pores
    • Redness for 24–48 hours
  • LED light therapy Calms inflammation and supports healing 30 min

    Painless light treatment that works well on its own or straight after a peel or microneedling.

    • Soothes redness and breakouts
    • Speeds up recovery
    • No downtime
  • Anti-wrinkle injections Softens expression lines on the forehead and eyes 30 min

    Small, precise doses that relax the muscles behind expression lines. The aim is for you to look rested, not frozen.

    • Results appear over 7–14 days
    • Lasts around 3–4 months
    • Review appointment included
  • Dermal fillers Restores volume to lips, cheeks and jawline 45 min

    Hyaluronic acid filler placed to balance proportions and replace lost volume. Always discussed in detail before we begin.

    • Immediate, natural-looking results
    • Lasts 6–12 months depending on area
    • Possible swelling for a few days
